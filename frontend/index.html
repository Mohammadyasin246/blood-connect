🩸 How BloodConnect Works

🧑

1. Recipient Requests

Patient enters required blood group, hospital location, and urgency (Normal, Urgent, Emergency).

⚡

2. Proximity Matching

The server computes great-circle distances via the Haversine formula and checks ABO/Rh compatibility.

🔔

3. Instant Alert & Accept

Nearest available donors receive high-priority alerts with a 1-tap Accept button, ensuring rapid response.

🛡️ Medical Safety Rules

  • ✅ 90-Day Safe Interval: Safe donation recovery period enforced automatically.
  • ✅ Privacy Protection: Donors' private mobile numbers are masked by default.
  • ✅ Hospital Verification: Hospital verification ensures clinical compliance.
  • ✅ Official Certificates: Automated appreciation certificate upon completion.
